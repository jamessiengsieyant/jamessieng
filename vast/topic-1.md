# Topic 1 — Capitalize or Expense?

Two real situations where I had to make, or defend, that call.

## Example 1 — ForensisGroup, Salesforce

The technical spine: a Salesforce subscription is a Cloud Computing Arrangement under ASC 350-40 (as amended by ASU 2018-15) — the subscription fee itself is never an asset, it's always a straight period expense, like rent. The real judgment call lives in the *implementation* costs: configuration, customization, and testing get capitalized as a prepaid asset and expensed ratably over the contract term; training and data conversion get expensed immediately, even though they feel like "setup work" too.

> NEED FROM JAMES: did ForensisGroup pay a consultant/agency for implementation, or was this your own configuration work with no separately billed cost? What was the actual dollar scale? What did you personally decide or recommend, and what did the firm end up doing?
